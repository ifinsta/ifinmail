## What is new in 1.1.0

- **Updates arrive by themselves.** ifinmail now checks your own server for new versions, tells you in the
  inbox and in Settings, and installs them after you confirm. Important fixes can be marked required.
- **Polished everywhere.** Refined spacing, type and colour across every screen, with a proper dark theme
  and larger, clearer touch targets.
- **Français.** The app follows your device language in English and French.
- **Accessibility.** Screen-reader labels and contrast were checked on every primary screen.
- **Sturdier release build.** Smaller, faster app with the security checks Android expects.
